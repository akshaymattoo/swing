We’re here to help

Support

Having trouble starting your sweat? Send us a note and we’ll help you get moving again.

Email Swing support

What to include

Your iPhone model, iOS version, Swing app version, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot helps when available.

Response time

We aim to respond within two to three business days during the beta. Please do not include private health or financial information.

Quick fixes

  • If a YouTube workout does not open, check your internet connection and try the link again.
  • If audio is quiet, check media volume and confirm your phone is not connected to another speaker or headset.
  • If the app appears stuck, close Swing, reopen it, and retry the action.
  • If a problem continues, include the workout name and the screen where it happened in your email.

Your workout data

Created workouts and history are stored locally on your device. Swing currently has no account or cloud restore. Removing the app may permanently remove that information.
