Plain-language policy

Privacy at Swing

Your workouts belong to you. Swing keeps your workout library and history on your device and collects only limited analytics needed to improve the app and website.

At a glance: no account, no ads, no sale of personal information, no precise location, and no cross-app tracking.

Information stored on your device

Swing stores your created and saved workouts, workout history, and app preferences in a local SQLite database on your device. This information is not uploaded to a Swing account or cloud database. Deleting the app generally deletes this local data, subject to your device and backup settings.

Analytics and diagnostics

Swing uses PostHog to understand how the app and website are used and to improve reliability. App analytics may include screen views, button interactions, workout equipment and intensity selections, workout started or completed events, app version, device type, operating system, country or region derived from network information, anonymous identifiers, and technical error logs.

Website analytics includes page views and named actions such as opening support, the feedback form, or the App Store. Website analytics runs in cookieless mode, does not identify visitors, and does not use session replay or broad automatic click capture.

We do not use PostHog for advertising or cross-app tracking, and we do not intentionally send workout names you type, your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, health records, or payment information. Analytics is retained only as long as reasonably needed to understand product performance and troubleshoot issues, subject to our PostHog workspace settings.

External services

Some Workout of the Day sessions open YouTube. The support page can open a Google feedback form. When you follow an external link, that service’s own privacy policy and terms apply. PostHog processes limited analytics on our behalf.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to provide. We use it only to respond, investigate the issue, and improve Swing. Please do not send sensitive health or financial information.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. We share limited data only with service providers that help operate and analyze Swing, when legally required, or to protect users and the service.

Children

Swing is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can stop future app analytics by uninstalling Swing. Website analytics does not store identifiers in cookies or browser storage. You may contact us to ask questions or request deletion of support information. Because analytics is anonymous, we may not be able to connect an analytics record to a particular person.

Exercise safety

Swing provides general fitness information and is not medical advice. Exercise within your abilities and consult a qualified professional when appropriate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Swing evolves. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
